# Topic: characters

Measure the characters, against ours.
- Proportions: height in head-heights, head shape (h/w ratio), limb thickness vs head width.
- Faces: eye size and spacing, mouth, expression range; colour of skin and outline (hex).
- Outline: thickness in px at 1080 wide, colour, thicker on one side?
- How they sit in the world: flat cards or 3D, lit by the scene light, shadows cast.
- Acting: how much they move per shot (walk bob, lean, gestures, blinks: frequency).
- Side characters: how many per shot, how they react on peak beats.
Answer: numbers and hex only, target vs ours, ≤200 words.
