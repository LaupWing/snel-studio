# Topic: light

Measure how the reference is lit, against ours.
- Key light: direction (azimuth/elevation estimate), colour (hex), hardness of shadows.
- Fill and ambient: how dark the shade side gets (luma), its colour.
- Cast shadows: present on characters and props? length, softness, contact shadow under feet.
- Rim light, bloom, glow: where, how strong.
- Light changes over a video and on which beats.
Answer: numbers and hex only, target vs ours, ≤200 words.
